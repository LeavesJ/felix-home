## What needs Docker, a model surface, the platform or the founder

Sandbox first execution and behaviour observation, the spec-conformant T2 and
behaviour receipts need Docker. Open-world obligation discovery at depth and
dispute pathway 2's proposal need a model surface. Scoped leases and
revocation need a platform that activates a capability mid-session, which
Claude Code does not (`lib/ready.sh:5-12`). Step 9's peer consumption and
signature need a peer and a key. The founder decides the discovery schedule,
arming qualification on elenchus and veldra, the risk-envelope values, and
turning Docker on.
